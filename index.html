The Comprehensive 13.8 Billion Year Cosmic Evolution Timeline

ĀDI is an interactive 3D WebGL educational experience detailing the history of the universe from the initial cosmic singularity to the cyberpunk technological future.

Era 0: The Void (Before Time and Space)

In the beginning, there was nothing. Absolute silence, zero dimensions, and infinite potential before the dawn of spacetime.

Era 1: The Singularity (13.8 Billion Years Ago)

The primordial point of infinite density and temperature. Witness the quantum gravitational fluctuations triggering the Big Bang expansion.

Era 2: The Big Bang & Inflation (13.79 Billion Years Ago)

Cosmic inflation expands spacetime faster than light. Subatomic particles form as the universe rapidly cools from trillions of degrees.

Era 3: First Stars & Reionization (13.6 Billion Years Ago)

Gravity condenses primordial hydrogen and helium into Population III supermassive stars, igniting nuclear fusion and illuminating the cosmic dark ages.

Era 4: The Cosmic Web & Galaxies (10 Billion Years Ago)

Dark matter filaments sculpt the universe into vast galactic superclusters, nebulae, and spiral galaxies like the Milky Way.

Era 5: Birth of the Solar System (4.6 Billion Years Ago)

A swirling solar nebula collapses to form our Sun, while protoplanetary dust disks coalesce into rocky terrestrial planets and gas giants.

Era 6: Formation of Earth & The Moon (4.5 Billion Years Ago)

A planetary collision between proto-Earth and Theia forms the Moon. Molten magma oceans cool to form the first terrestrial crust and oceans.

Era 7: Primordial Life & The Cambrian Explosion (540 Million Years Ago)

Deep-sea hydrothermal vents give birth to RNA and single-celled organisms, culminating in the Cambrian explosion of complex multicellular marine biodiversity.

Era 8: Reign of the Dinosaurs (250 - 66 Million Years Ago)

Mesozoic era megafauna rule lush green terrestrial ecosystems. Tyrannosaurus rex, Velociraptor, Triceratops, and Pterodactyls thrive until the asteroid impact.

Era 9: Human Evolution & Consciousness (300,000 Years Ago)

The rise of Homo sapiens. Genetic evolution and DNA double-helix structures give birth to neural networks, language, culture, and technological civilization.

Era 10: The Cyberpunk Technological Future

Humanity expands into orbital space stations, artificial intelligence, cybernetic enhancements, and advanced planetary megacities.

Era 11: The Unknown World & Beyond

The ultimate destiny of intelligence and cosmic evolution. The beginning is the end, and the end is the beginning of a new universal cycle.
